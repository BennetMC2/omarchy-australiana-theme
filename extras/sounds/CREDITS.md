# Kookaburra welcome

Source: [LaughingKookaburra.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LaughingKookaburra.ogg) by Kuco, recorded in southwest Australia, December 2007.

The recording was released into the public domain by its creator. Australiana uses a 3.2-second excerpt starting at 3 seconds, with reduced volume and short fades. Playback is further attenuated to 35% and respects system mute.
